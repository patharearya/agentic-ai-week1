The semester examination form submission deadline is 18 October 2026.
Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances.
The examination fee is ₹1500 for regular students.
Late fee is ₹500 extra.
Students must submit the form online through the official college portal.
Semester results will be announced on 30 November 2026.

Students should keep a copy of the submitted form for reference.
Contact the examination office for any queries before the deadline.
Fee once paid is non-refundable under any circumstances.
The examination will be held in the college main hall.
Roll numbers will be displayed on the college notice board one week before exams.
